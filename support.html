Quick answers

How does the 100m estimate work?

The App uses the distance and time you provide, then adjusts the result using the context you select. Detailed mode can account for factors such as start type, timing method, wind, surface, footwear, altitude, age, and sex. It is an estimate—not an official race time or medical assessment.

Do I need a wearable or GPS?

No. You only need a sprint distance and a measured time. Accuracy depends on the quality of the input and timing method.

Why are online features unavailable?

Check your connection, temporarily disable any network filtering, and try again. Core calculation features can still work while some leaderboards, rewards, purchases, and sync features require internet access.

How do I change units or language?

Open Profile in the App. You can select distance and speed units, calculation defaults, language, sound, haptics, and performance settings.

How do I remove my data?

Open Profile and choose “Delete account and cloud data”, or use our account deletion page.

Purchases and rewards

For a missing non-consumable Premium entitlement, open Profile and choose Restore Purchases while signed into the same Apple or Google store account. Consumable Spike packs are not restorable after they have been delivered.

If a purchase was charged but not delivered, restart the App and try again. If it still does not appear, contact support with the date, product name, platform, and the store’s order reference. Do not send your full payment details.

Billing and refunds are handled by the store where you bought the item. Use Apple’s or Google Play’s purchase support for a refund request.

Privacy and safety

Read the Privacy Policy for details about data handling. How Fast Am I? estimates sporting performance for information and entertainment; it does not provide medical, training, or safety advice. Sprint within your ability and use an appropriate surface and supervision.
