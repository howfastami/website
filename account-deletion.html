Delete in the App

This is the fastest and most reliable option because the App can securely identify your anonymous account.

  1. Open How Fast Am I? on the device linked to your account.
  2. Open the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll down and select Delete account and cloud data.
  4. Read the confirmation and choose Delete account.
  5. Keep the App open and connected while deletion completes. It may take a few minutes.

Deletion is permanent. It cannot be undone.

Request deletion outside the App

If you cannot access the App, email our privacy team using the button below. Use the subject “Account deletion request”. Include your leaderboard username (if you created one), country, device platform, and any other non-sensitive details that can help us locate the anonymous account. Do not send payment-card information.

Request account deletionsupport@howfastami.app

Because accounts are anonymous, we may ask for additional verification or direct you to confirm from the linked device. We aim to acknowledge requests within 3 business days and complete verified requests within 30 days, unless law permits or requires longer.

What is deleted

Deletion removes the account’s cloud profile, username reservation, leaderboard entries, squad and athlete ownership, progression, race submissions and personal bests, balances, tickets, badges, reward records associated with the account, and Firebase anonymous authentication account. The App also clears locally stored profile, history, cache, and gameplay data on the device that completes deletion.

What may remain

Purchase and rewarded-ad transaction identifiers may remain in de-identified form so the same transaction cannot be claimed more than once. App-store transaction records remain with Apple or Google under their policies. Backups and logs may persist temporarily before routine deletion, and information may be retained if required by law or necessary to resolve fraud, security, or legal issues.

Deleting the account does not cancel or refund purchases. A non-consumable Premium entitlement may be restorable from the same store account if you start again.
