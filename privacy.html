Your data, explained plainly

Privacy policy.

This policy explains what How Fast Am I? collects, why it is used, who processes it, and the choices available to you.

Effective: 27 November 2026 · Last updated: 27 November 2026

On this pageScopeData we handleHow we use itService providersRetentionYour choicesChildrenContact

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the How Fast Am I? mobile application for iOS and Android (the “App”) and this website, operated by How Fast Am I (“we”, “us”, or “our”).

The App creates an anonymous technical account so online game features can work without asking for your email address or password.

2. Data we handle

Information you choose to provide

  • Profile details such as display name, leaderboard username, country, age, and sex.
  • Sprint inputs and results, including distance, time, timing method, start type, footwear, surface, weather, altitude, and calculated performance results.
  • Squad selections, athlete progression, game balances, race results, badges, and other gameplay activity.
  • Messages and diagnostic information you send when contacting support.

Information created automatically

  • An anonymous Firebase account identifier used to connect your cloud data to your installation.
  • Online-service records such as leaderboard submissions, transaction timestamps, rate-limit records, rewarded-ad completion records, and account-deletion status.
  • Device and network information processed by service providers, which may include IP address, device identifiers, app version, operating system, crash or performance information, and advertising information depending on your device settings and consent choices.
  • Purchase and entitlement information from Apple App Store or Google Play, such as product, transaction identifier, purchase status, and purchase date. We do not receive your full payment-card details.

Information stored on your device

Profile preferences, calculation history, app settings, cached content, and some gameplay state are stored locally. Deleting the App may remove local data that has not been synced.

3. How we use data

  • Provide the sprint calculator, saved history, squad, racing, leaderboard, reward, and purchase features you request.
  • Save and synchronise eligible game progress across App sessions.
  • Verify purchases and rewarded-ad completions, prevent duplicate rewards, detect abuse, secure the service, and enforce reasonable usage limits.
  • Operate, troubleshoot, maintain, and improve the App and respond to support requests.
  • Meet legal obligations and enforce our terms.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use the website to place advertising cookies or run third-party analytics.

4. Service providers

We share data only as needed to operate the App, comply with law, protect users, or complete a transaction. Providers may process data under their own terms and privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase — anonymous authentication, cloud database, server functions, App Check, and related hosting infrastructure.
  • Google AdMob — optional rewarded advertising. Ad personalisation and identifiers depend on region, consent, device settings, and Google’s policies.
  • Apple App Store and Google Play — app distribution, in-app purchase processing, entitlement verification, and fraud prevention.
  • Infrastructure and professional advisers — where reasonably necessary for security, legal compliance, or support.

These providers may process information outside your country. Where required, safeguards are used for international transfers.

5. Retention and security

We retain cloud account and gameplay data while your account is active or as needed to provide the App. When you delete your account, associated profile, leaderboard, squad, progress, and cloud gameplay data are deleted. Purchase or rewarded-ad transaction identifiers may be retained in de-identified form to prevent the same transaction being claimed twice. Information may also be retained where required by law, to resolve disputes, or protect the service.

We use access controls, authenticated requests, platform integrity checks, encrypted network transport, and other reasonable safeguards. No system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Update profile data: use the Profile screen in the App.
  • Advertising choices: use the consent and privacy controls shown by the App or your device settings. Rewarded ads are optional.
  • Delete your account: in the App, open Profile and select “Delete account and cloud data”, or follow the external deletion instructions.
  • Access or correction: contact us. We may need reasonable information to verify the anonymous account belongs to you.
  • Local data: use Reset Profile, delete individual history where available, or delete the App. Account deletion removes both eligible cloud data and App data on that device.

Your local law may provide additional access, deletion, objection, restriction, portability, or complaint rights. Contact us to make a request.

7. Children

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in their country). We do not knowingly ask children to provide an email address or real name. If you believe a child has provided personal information without appropriate consent, contact us so we can investigate and delete it.

8. Changes

We may update this policy as the App, providers, or legal requirements change. We will post the revised policy here and update the date above. Material changes may also be communicated in the App.

9. Contact

For support or privacy questions and requests, email support@howfastami.app.
