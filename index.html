How fast is a 4.50-second 40-yard dash?

A 4.50-second 40-yard dash averages 18.2 mph (29.3 km/h). That’s 40 yards (36.576 metres) divided by 4.50 seconds.

Average speed covers the whole sprint, including acceleration. Your peak speed requires a separate measurement. In the app, you can also estimate your 100m time using details such as your start, timing method, and running surface.
